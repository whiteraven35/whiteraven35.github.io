Link Dock for iOS

Save links now. Find them later.

Link Dock is a simple iOS app for saving links, organizing them with categories and notes, adding reminders, attaching selected media, and returning to useful pages when you need them.

Organize

Save URLs with titles, categories, notes, dates, and domains so important links stay easy to find.

Revisit

Set optional reminders and use search or filters to quickly return to saved content.

Private by Design

Your saved link data is stored on your device, with optional iCloud support when available.

Privacy Policy and Terms

Link Dock does not operate its own account system or server for saved links. For privacy questions, terms, or support requests, contact linkdock.request@gmail.com.
