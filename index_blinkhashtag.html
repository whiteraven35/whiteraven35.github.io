Hashtags in a blink — for iOS & iPadOS creators.

This is the official developer website for Blink Hashtag, a mobile app that helps users generate, copy, save, and manage hashtag ideas for social media content.

App Information

App name: Blink Hashtag

Platform: iOS & iPadOS

Bundle ID: com.blink.blinkhashtag

Category: Productivity

Distribution: Apple App Store

About the App

Blink Hashtag lets you:

  • Generate hashtag groups by topic, platform, style, and content goal
  • Copy results instantly for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and more
  • Save History and Favorites, with optional iCloud sync
  • Use Boost packs for quick hashtag inspiration
  • Remove rewarded ads with a one-time in-app purchase

Advertising

The free version of Blink Hashtag may show rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob before certain actions. Users who purchase Remove Ads will not see rewarded ads. Advertising practices are described in our Privacy Policy.
