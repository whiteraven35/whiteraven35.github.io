Story Keeper: Lie Tracker

A private, playful vault for saving personal story cards, exact words, reminders, people, importance levels, private notes, and optional photos.

  • Private story cards
  • Face ID lock support
  • Reminders and iCloud sync

Story Keeper stores your story cards locally on your device and may use iCloud sync when available. The app uses Google AdMob for rewarded ads and offers an optional one-time purchase to remove ads.
